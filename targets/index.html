Call sheet

Every publisher, ranked on evidence

The engine behind the first wave: every publisher cited in the dated sample, ranked on what was read about it. This ranks evidence, not readiness to buy. Move the weights to yours and the order changes. A signal not yet read is left out of that publisher’s total and maximum, never counted as zero.

Weights

With your weights, each publisher ranks by points earned over points available on its known signals. The weights are kept in the page address, so you can share them.

Citation share is the share of a vertical’s sampled probes whose answer cited the publisher. It is citation frequency on this one dated sample, not measured agent traffic. Full marks at one probe in four. Each publisher sits in the vertical where its share is highest. “Signals” counts how many of the five are known.
