Where I’d start, and where I wouldn’t

The call sheet ranks who AI answers cite. This page checks who could actually say yes. Each account was reviewed by hand against its current public pages, with every finding sourced and dated. Ready for discovery means worth a first conversation, not a qualified lead.

How accounts are chosen

Judgement, written down

  • Start from the call sheet, not a hunch, then check each candidate’s current site by hand.
  • A commercial route must be published before an account is ready for discovery: an advertise page, a commercial role, a partnerships route.
  • Editorial policy can rule an account out. Affiliate income is not the same as being open to sponsored placements.
  • Accounts are named only as far as a source supports. No parent company is assumed from a domain name.
  • robots.txt and CDN headers are not readiness. They show a declared crawler policy and the edge a site sits behind, not agent traffic, deployment access or willingness to pilot.
  • Fewer verified accounts beat a full quota. Holds and exclusions are shown because they are the judgement.
