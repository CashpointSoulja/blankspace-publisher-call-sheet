The assumption

This is built on one assumption: supply is the constraint.

From the job description and interviews, I’ve assumed that getting live on publisher sites is what limits growth right now. Everything here is aimed at that.

Scoring

How a publisher earns a place

Five signals, 100 points. Every point traces to a file you can open. A signal not yet read is left out of the total and the maximum. It is never counted as zero. So far only citation share is collected for the sample, so the call sheet scores out of 40. The live check reads the other four for any publisher.

Scoring 100 points

  • Citation share

    Dated sample
    40

    Share of a vertical’s sampled probes whose answer cited the publisher. Full marks at one in four. Citation frequency on this sample, not measured traffic.

  • Placement fit

    Live
    20

    Share of sitemap pages that are roundups, comparisons or reviews, plus ItemList, Product or Review markup. These are the pages a list placement fits into.

  • Programmatic stack

    Live
    15

    ads.txt published, five or more ad systems listed, header bidding in the initial HTML.

  • Commerce monetisation

    Live
    15

    Affiliate networks found in page links. A publisher already earning from buying intent understands the pitch quickly.

  • Agent reachability

    Live
    10

    Search and user-triggered AI agents not disallowed in robots.txt. This is declared policy, not observed behaviour.

  • CDN

    Live
    Shown, not scored

    Read from response headers. Which CDNs blankspace deploys on is unknown to me, so it doesn’t move the score.

The weights are my opinion. On the call sheet you can move them to yours and watch the order change.
